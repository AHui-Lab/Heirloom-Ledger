# Video Demo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Warm cinematic editorial case study for a Chinese antique-shop narrative game. The frame should feel like dark timber, aged paper, amber light, and careful evidence handling. Use generous negative space, restrained gold accents, soft camera-like pushes, and page-turn transitions. The tone is quiet and premium rather than flashy: an intimate craft story presented with clear product-demo structure.

## Colors

- `#171512` — ink-black background
- `#282119` — dark wood panel
- `#c89f55` — antique gold accent
- `#e4c98f` — warm headline text
- `#c6b89f` — readable secondary text

## Typography

- Display / story voice: `Georgia, serif`
- Labels / system voice: `Consolas, monospace`

## Motion

Use slow push-ins, gentle vertical reveals, and warm-gold page-turn transitions. Every scene enters with a visible but restrained motion cue. No glitch, hard cuts, neon gradients, or excessive kinetic type.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not show API configuration, keys, logs, source code, or internal documents.
- Do not imply that test dialogue is final live-model output.
- Do not use flashy glitch transitions or unreadably small subtitles.
- Do not claim infinite generation or infinite memory.
